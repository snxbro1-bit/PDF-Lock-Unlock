How to Lock a PDF

Protect your PDF with a password before sharing or storing it.

  1. Upload your PDF file.
  2. Enter a password and confirm it.
  3. Click Lock & Download PDF.
  4. Download your password-protected PDF.

How to Unlock a PDF

Remove password protection from a PDF when you know the existing password.

  1. Upload your locked PDF.
  2. Enter the current PDF password.
  3. Click Unlock & Download PDF.
  4. Download the unlocked PDF.
🔒 Your PDF files are processed directly in your browser. Files are not uploaded to a server.
